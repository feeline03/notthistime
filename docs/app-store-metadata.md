## Recommended Description

```text
Pause before a craving becomes action.

Not This Time is a simple 3-minute craving timer for the moment you want to eat but wish you could wait first.

When food cravings hit, they can feel urgent. This app helps you slow down, notice the urge, and give it a few minutes before you decide what to do next.

----

How it works:
- Choose what you’re craving
- Start a 3-minute pause
- Listen to the ticking timer
- Stay with the craving as it rises and fades
- Decide again when the timer ends

----

Cravings are not always the same as hunger.

Sometimes they come from stress, emotion, habit, boredom, or late-night snack impulses. Not This Time gives you a small space between the urge and the action.

----

Useful for moments like:

- Stress eating

- Wanting more control before you eat
```

## Why This Version

- Opens with the product action, not a strong claim.
- Replaces "stop the urge to binge eat" with "pause before a craving becomes action" to reduce overclaim risk.
- Keeps `binge eating`, `overeating`, `emotional eating`, `stress eating`, `late-night snacking`, and `craving timer` language naturally in the description.
- Clarifies the free and Pro boundary.
- Avoids medical treatment claims, weight-loss guarantees, fake social proof, and shame-heavy copy.
