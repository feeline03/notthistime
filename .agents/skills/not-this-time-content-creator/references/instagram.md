# Instagram Copy Adapter

Use for Instagram captions and carousel text.

## Goal

Optimize for save/share, emotional recognition, and soft discovery. Instagram copy should feel visual and personal even when only text is requested.

## Format

Short-form video text:

- Default to a 5-7 second video or static / near-static visual with screen text.
- Keep screen text to one moment or one unresolved feeling.
- Aim for roughly 8-16 English words and usually one or two short lines.
- Extend only when the extra words materially improve recognition or curiosity.
- Put context, CTA, product mention, and hashtags in the caption.

Caption:

- Opening line.
- 3-6 short paragraphs.
- Soft CTA.
- 5-10 hashtags when useful.

Carousel text:

- 5-8 slides.
- Each slide has one clear sentence or short cluster.
- First slide must work like a cover hook.
- Last slide ends with one concrete action or question.

## Style

- More polished than Xiaohongshu, but not inspirational.
- Use simple English for overseas audiences.
- Prefer human scenes over advice.
- Avoid too many hashtags and wellness clichés.

## English Voice

Keep English plain and intimate:

- "I wasn't hungry. I was tired."
- "The hardest part is not the whole diet. It is the ten minutes before you snack."
- "I wait three minutes before I decide."

Avoid:

- "Unlock your best self."
- "Transform your relationship with food."
- "Build a sustainable wellness lifestyle."

## Product CTA

Use soft CTAs:

- "I built Not This Time for that exact moment."
- "It is just a 3-minute pause before you decide."
- "Search Not This Time on the App Store if you need that pause too."

Do not overuse download language.
