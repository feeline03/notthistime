# Medium Copy Adapter

Use for Medium article outlines and drafts.

## Goal

Optimize for trust, first-hand experience, thoughtful value, and human writing. Medium is not a short-video platform; do not use shouty hooks or formulaic listicles.

## Format

Produce:

- 3 title options.
- 1 subtitle.
- Outline.
- Full draft or section draft depending on user request.
- Soft CTA at the end if relevant.

## Structure

1. Personal scene: one real moment.
2. Tension: why the usual explanation failed.
3. Discovery: what Zhuzi noticed through eating control, keto/low-carb, product building, or user comments.
4. Plain explanation: translate any concept into human language.
5. Practical shift: what to try in the craving moment.
6. Reflection: what remains unresolved or worth testing.
7. Soft app mention only if natural.

## Style

- Use complete paragraphs, but keep them readable.
- Keep the writing personal and specific.
- Do not over-optimize for clickbait.
- Do not make the article a sales pitch.
- Avoid generic AI article openings such as "In today's fast-paced world".

## English Voice

Write like a founder essay from lived experience:

- "I used to think the problem was sugar. Then I realized I was studying the wrong thing."
- "The craving did not disappear because I became disciplined. It changed when I stopped treating it as hunger."

## CTA

Use one soft close:

- "I am building Not This Time around this tiny pause."
- "It is not a cure. It is a tool for the moment before action."

Do not promise treatment, weight loss, or recovery.
