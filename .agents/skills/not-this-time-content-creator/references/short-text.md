# Threads And X Copy Adapter

Use for Threads and X text-only posts.

## Goal

Optimize for fast recognition, conversation, reposts, and replies. Do not sound like a polished brand account.

## Format Options

Produce only the formats requested. If unspecified, provide:

- 3 single-post options.
- 1 short thread, 3-6 posts.
- 3 reply prompts or follow-up questions.

## Style

Threads:

- More conversational.
- A little more vulnerable.
- One opinion plus one personal detail works well.
- End with a question that invites a real reply.

X:

- Sharper first line.
- Clearer argument progression.
- Avoid vague diary tone.
- Thread should make one point, not become an essay.

## Structure

Single post:

1. Concrete moment or contrarian line.
2. Personal observation.
3. One plain insight.
4. Specific question or soft CTA.

Thread:

1. Hook: one uncomfortable or recognizable truth.
2. Scene: what happens in the body or behavior.
3. Reframe: what it may actually mean.
4. Small move: what Zhuzi tries now.
5. Question: ask for one concrete user moment.

## Guardrails

- Do not use generic founder-post language.
- Do not make the app the hero.
- Do not over-explain psychology.
- Avoid motivational slogans.
- Keep the first line independently strong.
