Not This Time · Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 13, 2026

Welcome to “I Resisted”.

“I Resisted” is a local-first app designed to help users manage food cravings and impulse control through mindfulness and timed intervention techniques.

We value your privacy and minimize data collection whenever possible.

Most of your data remains stored locally on your device. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

1. Data Collection

This app does not:

  • create user accounts
  • require user login
  • collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, or phone number)
  • track users for advertising purposes
  • sell user data
  • use third-party advertising SDKs

2. Anonymous Usage Analytics

To help us improve app stability and user experience, we collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • feature usage frequency
  • crash reports and performance data
  • general interaction behaviors (e.g., button taps, session duration)

This data is associated only with an anonymous identifier (UUID / IDFV) and cannot be used to identify you personally.

These analytics are uploaded securely to our server solely for product improvement and are never shared with advertisers or third parties.

3. Local Storage

To support app functionality, the app may locally store:

  • countdown records
  • intervention history
  • user preferences
  • audio playback settings
  • statistics and success records

This information remains on your device and is not accessible to the developer unless uploaded as part of anonymous analytics described above.

4. Notification Permission

The app may request notification permission in order to provide:

  • countdown reminders
  • intervention completion alerts
  • support for focus and self-control workflows

Notifications are generated locally on your device. No personal information is collected through notification features.

5. Background Audio

To support meditation guidance, white noise playback, and lock screen audio controls, the app may continue audio playback while running in the background.

No personal data is collected or transmitted during audio playback.

6. Third-Party Services

This app currently does not integrate third-party advertising or tracking services.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published within the app or related pages.

9. Contact

我忍住啦 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 5 月 13 日

欢迎使用「我忍住啦」。

「我忍住啦」是一款帮助用户进行饮食冲动管理与自我干预的本地应用。 我们高度重视您的隐私，并尽可能减少数据收集。

绝大多数数据均存储在您的设备本地。我们不会收集您的姓名、手机号、电子邮箱等个人身份信息。

1. 数据收集说明

「我忍住啦」：

  • 不创建用户账户
  • 不要求用户登录
  • 不收集个人身份信息（如姓名、邮箱、手机号）
  • 不进行广告追踪
  • 不出售用户数据
  • 不使用第三方广告 SDK

2. 匿名使用数据分析

为了持续优化产品体验与应用稳定性，我们会收集以下匿名使用数据：

  • 功能使用频率
  • 崩溃日志与性能数据
  • 通用交互行为（如按钮点击、使用时长）

这些数据仅与匿名设备标识（UUID / IDFV）关联，无法用于识别您的真实身份。

相关数据会安全上传至我们的服务器，仅用于产品改进，不会用于广告投放，也不会共享给第三方。

3. 本地数据存储

为了实现应用功能，本应用可能会在您的设备本地存储以下内容：

  • 倒计时记录
  • 干预历史
  • 用户偏好设置
  • 音频播放配置
  • 统计数据与成功记录

这些数据仅存储于您的设备中；除非作为前述匿名分析数据的一部分上传，否则开发者无法访问。

如果您卸载应用，本地数据可能会被系统删除。

4. 通知权限说明

本应用可能会请求系统通知权限，用于：

  • 倒计时提醒
  • 干预结束提醒
  • 帮助用户完成专注与自我控制流程

通知内容由系统在您的设备本地生成， 我们不会通过通知功能收集您的个人信息。

您可以随时在 iOS 系统设置中关闭通知权限。

5. 后台音频说明

为了支持冥想引导、白噪声播放及锁屏控制功能， 本应用可能会在后台持续播放音频。

音频播放过程不会收集或上传任何个人数据。

6. 第三方服务

本应用当前不接入第三方广告或用户追踪服务。

如未来功能发生变化，我们将同步更新本隐私政策。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不会主动收集儿童个人信息。

8. 隐私政策更新

我们可能会根据应用功能变化对本隐私政策进行更新。 更新后的版本将在应用内或相关页面中公布。
